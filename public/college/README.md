Drop photos in this folder (jpg, jpeg, png, webp, avif or gif) and they show up
in the "But College is More than Academics" section automatically.

- The file name becomes the photo's description, e.g. `purdue-football-game.jpg`
  -> "Purdue football game".
- Photos are shown in file-name order; prefix with numbers (`01-...`) to control it.
- iPhone HEIC photos don't display in browsers; export them as JPG first.
- Keep files reasonably small (under ~2 MB each); the site resizes them, but
  huge originals slow down builds and the first load.
